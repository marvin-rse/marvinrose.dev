I'm **Marvin Rose**, Cyber Security Architect at [glueckkanja AG](https://www.glueckkanja.com/). Day to day I design and operate security platforms built on the Microsoft stack — **Microsoft Sentinel**, **Defender XDR** and **Entra ID** — for organisations that want detections they can trust and costs they can explain.

This blog is where I write down what I learn along the way: how things really work under the hood, the KQL I reach for, and the mistakes worth avoiding.

- **Defender XDR** — advanced hunting, custom detections, incident handling
- **Entra ID** — Conditional Access, identity protection, workload identities
- **KQL** — queries you can paste and use
